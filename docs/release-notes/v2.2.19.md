## Fixes

- Stop automatic polling of unresolved video tasks after 24 hours. Tasks that remain missing after a five-minute creation grace period move to a persistent reconciliation state instead of retrying indefinitely.
- Preserve reserved funds and subscription quota while a task's outcome is unknown. Later confirmed completion settles once; confirmed failure releases the reservation once. Prepared charges continue recovering independently of the polling limit.
- Back off failed background polls up to 15 minutes while retaining the original task account and API key ownership checks.
