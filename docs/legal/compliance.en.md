# Compliance & Business Statement

**Last updated: 22 September 2026**

This statement explains the Site's business boundary and risk-control approach so that "API relay" is not mistaken for model provision, a content platform, subscription reselling or a licensed financial institution.

In short: we operate a developer-facing **AI API aggregation / gateway**. Customers prepay balance and call generative models through a unified API. We are **not** a reseller of third-party personal subscriptions or accounts, and we do **not** help anyone bypass upstream policies.

## 1. Business Positioning

We provide an AI API aggregation and developer platform within authorised channels: a unified, commonly compatible API, console, documentation and usage-based billing. Customers purchase platform API usage, not a physical good and not a third-party account.

## 2. Operating Entity and Payment

The party that provides the Service and receives payment is the operator identified in the website footer, checkout flow and in-site display. Customer payments purchase platform usage credits associated with the account. Contact us via in-site support.

## 3. Model Sources and Data Flow

Requests are received by the platform for authentication, billing and safety controls, forwarded to the official or otherwise authorised upstream API of the relevant model provider, and the response is returned to you. Models originate from or are provided through official APIs of major providers and their authorised distribution channels, which may include among others OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, Meta, Alibaba (Qwen), ByteDance and DeepSeek. Availability changes over time.

## 4. Services We Do Not Provide

To avoid misunderstanding, we do not offer, and prohibit use of the Service for:

- **Account sharing** — we do not rent, resell or pool third-party AI accounts, and we do not use customers' personal ChatGPT, Claude or similar subscriptions to provide the Service;
- **Subscription top-up reselling** — we do not top up or renew third-party personal subscriptions on a customer's behalf;
- **Circumvention of upstream restrictions** — we do not help bypass geographic restrictions, bans, quotas or rate limits;
- **Unofficial access** — we do not provide restricted services through unofficial or unauthorised means.

Customers must comply with each upstream provider's terms. Prohibited content is set out in the Acceptable Use Policy.

## 5. Trademarks and Authorisation

Names such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI, and any model names, are used only to identify capabilities accessible through the platform. Trademarks belong to their owners; use does not imply partnership, sponsorship or endorsement. Commercial access is through official upstream APIs and authorised channels. If a clear commercial authorisation path is not available we may adjust presentation or withdraw the capability.

## 6. Risk-Control Approach

We follow "default-deny for high risk, observable, interruptible": group and model allowlists, rate limits, balance pre-checks, anomaly detection, configurable content-safety policy, and the right to freeze abuse and chargebacks. We do not promise real-time human review of every request. Final compliance responsibility sits with the user.

## 7. Contact

Compliance, business or partnership enquiries should go through in-site support. This statement is not legal advice. If it conflicts with the formal terms, the then-current Terms of Service and related policies prevail.
