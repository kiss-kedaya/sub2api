# Supported Countries and Regions

**Last updated: 22 September 2026**

This page explains geographic availability of the Service and restrictions that upstream models may impose. It supplements the "Restricted Models" section of the Terms of Service.

## 1. Platform Access

The Website and console are reachable from most regions, but we do not guarantee continuous access in any country. Network blocking, local law or infrastructure limits may prevent use. You must ensure that using the Service does not violate the law of your location.

## 2. Model and Upstream Restrictions

Some model providers do not allow access from particular countries, regions, industries or entities. Even if you can sign in, the corresponding model may still be refused, degraded or return an upstream error. Availability is as shown in the console, the model list and actual upstream responses.

## 3. No Circumvention

You must not use VPNs, proxies, false location or other means to bypass geographic, entity or use-case restrictions set by us or by upstream providers. We may restrict models, freeze the account and withhold related balance if we find circumvention.

## 4. Your Responsibility

Before offering the Service to your customers or deploying it in a given market, review local generative-AI regulation, upstream territorial terms, data-export rules and content-moderation duties. A model's appearance on the list is not a warranty that it may be used lawfully in your target market.

## 5. Changes

Provider lists, model catalogues and territorial policies may change at any time. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the then-current restrictions. Contact in-site support with questions.
