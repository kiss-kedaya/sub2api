# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Last updated: 22 September 2026**

This Agreement is entered into between the operator of this Site (the "Processor") and the customer that accepts the Terms of Service (the "Customer" or "Controller") and is incorporated into the Terms of Service.

It applies if you accept the Terms on behalf of an organisation or use the Service for commercial or for-profit purposes. Personal use is governed by the Privacy Policy.

## 1. Definitions

"Personal Data" means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. "Processing" includes collection, storage, use, transmission, disclosure, restriction, erasure and destruction. "Controller" determines purposes and means; "Processor" processes on the Controller's behalf; "Sub-processor" is a third party engaged by the Processor; "Data Breach" is a security incident leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of or access to Personal Data. "Applicable Data Protection Law" means all laws that apply to the Processing, which may include the GDPR where it applies.

## 2. Scope and Roles

This Agreement applies where we process Personal Data on the Customer's behalf:

- **Customer (Controller)** decides which data is sent to the API and for which business purposes;
- **the Site (Processor)** transmits, formats and returns data solely on the Customer's instructions to provide API routing, billing and risk control, and does not determine the purposes itself.

Account registration, billing and security logs that we process as an independent controller are governed by the Privacy Policy, not by the "processing only on instructions" clauses of this DPA.

## 3. Details of Processing

- Nature: transmission, routing, formatting and return via API;
- Purpose: LLM API aggregation and routing for the Customer;
- Categories of Personal Data: Input submitted through the API (which may contain end-user Personal Data), account identifiers, API usage metadata;
- Data subjects: the Customer's end users, employees or authorised users;
- Duration: the service term, any period the Customer instructs in writing, and any period required by law.

## 4. Our Obligations

As Processor we will:

- process Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions (including API requests) and this Agreement and the Terms; where Processing is required by law we will inform the Customer if permitted;
- ensure authorised persons are under confidentiality obligations;
- implement technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk;
- bind Sub-processors as in Clause 6;
- assist the Customer, so far as reasonably practicable, with data-subject requests and with security, breach notification and impact-assessment duties;
- delete or return Processor-role Personal Data after the Service ends, unless law requires retention;
- make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance and contribute to audits on reasonable notice.

## 5. Security Measures

These include TLS for API traffic; least-privilege access to systems storing Personal Data; tenant isolation; encryption of sensitive credentials at rest; continuous security monitoring; and regular internal review.

## 6. Sub-processors

You authorise us to engage Sub-processors necessary to provide the Service, including upstream model providers, payment channels, hosting, CDN, email and CAPTCHA. We require confidentiality and security obligations no less protective than this Agreement. Intelligent routing requires forwarding encrypted API payloads to the upstream interface specified in your request. A list of current Sub-processors is available via in-site support.

We will give reasonable prior notice of intended additions or replacements so you may raise a reasonable objection. The parties will negotiate in good faith.

## 7. Data Breach Notification

After becoming aware of a Data Breach affecting data entrusted to us we will notify you in writing without undue delay (targeting 72 hours) and, to the extent available, describe the nature of the breach, the approximate categories and numbers of data subjects and records, likely consequences, and measures taken or proposed. Information may be provided in phases. We will assist you, so far as reasonably practicable, with notifications to authorities and data subjects.

## 8. Data Subject Rights

We will, so far as reasonably practicable, assist the Customer in responding to requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection.

## 9. Retention and Deletion

We retain Personal Data only as long as needed to provide the Service or as required by law. By default, if prompt logging is not enabled, we do not keep Input/Output body content for training after a call completes. On termination or written request we will within a reasonable period (not exceeding thirty days) delete or return then delete Processor-role Personal Data; legally required copies will be isolated and not processed for other purposes.

## 10. Cross-Border Transfers

Personal Data may be processed where our servers or upstream providers are located. Where a transfer must be restricted under Applicable Data Protection Law we will use appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses, an adequacy decision or another recognised mechanism).

## 11. Audit

On at least thirty days' prior notice, and generally no more than once a year, you may audit how we process Personal Data entrusted by you, yourself or through an independent auditor bound by confidentiality. You bear the cost; we will cooperate reasonably.

## 12. Precedence, Law and Term

On Processor-role personal-data protection this DPA prevails over conflicting Terms of Service. It takes effect when you accept the Terms and continues until the service relationship ends; deletion or return obligations survive until performed. Dispute resolution follows the Terms of Service and does not require a particular foreign arbitral institution.
