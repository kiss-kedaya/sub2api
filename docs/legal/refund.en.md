# Refund Policy

**Last updated: 22 September 2026**

This Policy explains refund rules for prepaid balance, plan quota and payment channels, and forms part of the Terms of Service. Read it before topping up or consuming credits.

## 1. About Prepaid Balance

Accessing the Service or certain features requires prepaid balance or plan quota associated with your account. Payment-channel fees may also apply. You will have an opportunity to review the amount before payment. Any minimum or maximum purchase amount is as shown on the top-up page at the time.

## 2. Consumed Fees

The following are **non-refundable**:

- calls successfully forwarded that incurred upstream cost, whether or not the output met expectations;
- failures caused by your Input, model choice, timeout, client disconnect or your own network, if the upstream still billed;
- used quota when the account is restricted, frozen or closed for breach of the Acceptable Use Policy or Terms of Service.

## 3. Unused Balance

Unused balance may be submitted for refund in the console if that payment channel supports user refunds. We will check whether the order was paid, whether it was partly consumed, whether the channel can reverse the payment, and whether there is a chargeback, abuse or dispute.

Only the unused portion is refunded if approved. Channel fees, on-chain miner fees and FX differences are usually borne by you. Arrival time depends on the channel; we cannot guarantee instant receipt.

## 4. USDT and Crypto

> USDT and other crypto top-ups are **in principle non-refundable** once confirmed on-chain, without exception. Make sure you understand this before paying with crypto. You warrant that the crypto and its source of funds are lawful and not the proceeds of crime.

Losses from sending the wrong amount, network or address are yours. If a platform fault causes a duplicate credit we may correct the ledger; that is not a promise of an on-chain reversal.

## 5. Auto Top-Up

If you enable auto top-up you authorise us to charge your selected method when balance falls below the threshold. Auto-purchased credits follow the same refund rules as manual purchases. You may disable auto top-up at any time; it does not unwind charges already initiated.

## 6. Balance on Account Termination

- **Termination initiated by you**: unused balance that the channel can reverse may be requested under this Policy; crypto remains in principle non-refundable.
- **Termination for breach**: remaining and used quota are not refunded.

## 7. Limited Technical Exceptions

After verifying system logs we may reverse or credit balance in these limited cases:

- **Duplicate platform charges** caused by our server fault or routing anomaly;
- **Complete unavailability** on our side that makes purchased quota wholly unusable during its validity, in which case we will compensate reasonably;
- **Unauthorised top-up**: contact support promptly with a written account and evidence so we can help initiate a reversal.

## 8. Fee Changes

Price or fee changes will be notified in-product or by other reasonable means. If you do not accept them you may stop purchasing; unused balance remains subject to this Policy.

## 9. Timing and Contact

Approved refunds go back to the original method when the channel allows. Card and other channel timings are set by the channel. Contact in-site support with the order number, payment method and reason.
